Built with photographers.

Every great photo in Focal is real, shared by its photographer, and credited with a link back. Exposure isn’t a paycheck, so Focal pays through the team program: a monthly share for every member your code brings in.

  • Your photos, teaching.
    Share your work and it becomes the inspiration and the applied example inside lessons and games: light, composition, timing. Generated images handle the drills and the don’ts. Every appearance of your photo carries your name and links back to you, so people who love it can find the person who made it.
  • Credit, always. Respect, always.
    Your work is never shown as a “what not to do.” We use stock and generated images to show negative examples and isolate concepts, never a real photographer’s submission. Your photos appear where they shine.
  • Get paid to grow the craft.
    Photographers should be paid for their work. That’s why Focal has a team program: anyone can join and get a referral code. Your code gives new members a discount on the paid plan, and you earn a monthly share for as long as your referral stays. Real, recurring support for your photography journey.
  • Open to everyone.
    You don’t need a following or a portfolio review. If you care about photography and want to help more people learn it, you belong on the team. Full program details when we launch.

First to hear when submissions and the team program open. No spam, ever.
