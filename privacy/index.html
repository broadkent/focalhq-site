Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Who we are

Focal is made by Focal HQ LLC, a Utah company ("Focal", "we", "us"). This policy explains what Focal collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Your account. You sign in with Google through our sign-in provider. We receive your name, your email address and an account ID. We never see your Google password. We use your email to check that you may use Focal, and we keep it with any photos you submit to the gallery and any written feedback you send.

Your learning and profile. Your lesson progress, XP and streak, the name you choose, your gear, your settings, the photos you save or like, your albums and your profile picture. Your profile picture is shrunk to a small copy before it is saved.

Your Insights history. Each time you use Describe or Compare, we keep the words and notes Focal gave you, and a small thumbnail of any photo you uploaded.

Photos you ask Focal to read. When you use Describe, Compare, a style check or a gear scan, the photo is sent to us and on to an AI service provider, with the reference photo in Compare. We do not save the photo itself. We keep only the result and the small thumbnail above.

Photos you submit to the gallery. If you choose Submit your photos, we keep the files as you send them, with any details your camera saved inside them, such as the camera, lens and exposure settings, and the place if your camera recorded it. We also keep the credit name and links you give, your account ID and email, and the gallery terms you agreed to.

Smart guide. Smart guide is off unless you turn it on. While it is on during a demo recording, your browser's speech service turns your voice into words. We never receive your voice. Your last few lines of words, the guide's steps and the page you are on are sent to us and on to an AI service provider, which suggests a short prompt. We do not save or log those words.

Camera and microphone in Record demo. Record demo uses your camera for a preview and pictures, and your microphone to show a sound level. Both stay on your device and are never uploaded. The one exception is Smart guide: while it is on, your browser's speech service hears your voice.

Usage counts. We count how many times a day your account reads photos, uses Smart guide, submits to the gallery and sends feedback, to keep these fair and affordable. Each count holds only a date and a number.

Cookies and browser storage. Focal sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in for up to 14 days. Our sign-in provider also keeps your sign-in in your browser's storage. Both are needed for Focal to work.

Request records. Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical details of each request, such as your IP address, your browser type and the time, to run and protect Focal.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Focal keeps the learning records described above, such as your progress, library and Insights history. It records your screen and taps only when you start Live feedback, and your voice only when you dictate a message or start Live feedback.

Feedback you choose to send

The Feedback button on Learn, Insights and Profile offers two ways to tell us what you think. Nothing in this section happens unless you start it.

Written feedback. If you send us a message, we keep the text, your account ID and email, the page you were on, the app version, and your screen size and browser. If you dictate it, the recording goes to a speech-to-text provider that turns it into text. We do not keep the recording.

Live feedback. If you start Live feedback and tap "Allow mic & camera", Focal records, until you tap Done:

  • your voice, including answers you speak to questions after a lesson;
  • small pictures of the app's screen, about once a second. They show whatever the app shows, such as your photos, your name and text you type;
  • where you tap, drag and scroll, and the pages, menus and settings you open or change;
  • where you look, worked out from your front camera on your phone. The camera video stays on your phone. Only numbers worked out from it are sent to us.

You can say no to the camera, or skip eye tracking, and still give voice feedback. A session also ends after 15 idle minutes, or after eight hours.

Where it goes. The recording goes to private storage at our cloud hosting provider. A speech-to-text provider then turns your voice into a transcript, and we build a replay of the session from the pictures, taps, voice and transcript. Only the Focal team can open it, and we use it only to improve Focal.

How long we keep it. We delete the raw voice recordings 30 days after upload. We keep the replay, which includes your voice, and the transcript, pictures, taps, eye-direction numbers and written messages only as long as we need them to improve Focal. We delete them sooner if you ask.

How we use it

To run Focal: to sign you in, keep your progress and library in sync across your devices, read your photos, coach you, and show your gallery photos as you agreed. We also use it to keep Focal secure and within its usage limits, to improve Focal from the feedback you choose to send, and to answer you when you write to us.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

We use these kinds of service providers. Each handles your data only to provide its service to us:

  • our sign-in provider, to sign you in;
  • our cloud hosting provider, which runs Focal and stores your account data, gallery photos and feedback;
  • an AI service provider, which reads photos and writes Smart guide prompts. Under its terms with us, it may not use them to train its models;
  • speech-to-text providers, which turn the voice in feedback you send into text.

When Smart guide is on, your browser's speech service turns your voice into words. That service belongs to your browser's maker, not to us, and its own privacy policy covers your voice.

We may also share information when the law requires it. If Focal HQ LLC is sold or merges with another company, your information may pass to the new owner, and this policy will keep applying to it.

What stays on your device

Your device keeps a copy of your lessons, history, saved photos, albums, gear, settings and profile picture, so Focal works quickly. Photos you read with Focal may be kept on your device as part of your history. Only a small thumbnail of each is ever synced to your account.

Signing out does not erase this copy. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data, synced library and history: until you delete them, or until your account is deleted.
  • Gallery submissions: until you ask us to remove them.
  • Feedback: as described under Feedback you choose to send.
  • Usage counts: as long as your account exists.
  • Photos you ask Focal to read, and Smart guide words: we do not keep them.
  • Request records: for a limited time, kept by our hosting provider.

Your choices

  • See it. Your data shows in the app: your profile, gear, saved photos, albums and history.
  • Change or remove parts of it. You can edit your name and gear, remove saved photos, delete albums and remove history items.
  • Remove gallery photos. Ask us at any time, and we will remove any photo you submitted.
  • Get a copy, or delete everything. Email us, and within 30 days we will send you a copy of your data, or delete your account, your feedback and the rest of your data.
  • Turn off Smart guide. Nothing is sent while it is off.
  • Live feedback. Starting is always your choice; you can tap "Not now". You can stop at any time with Done.
  • Camera and microphone. Your browser asks before Focal can use them. You can take that permission back in your browser's settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as to correct your data or to object to how we use it. Email us to use any of them.

Children

Focal is not for children under 13, or under 16 in the European Union. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under these ages. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
